Privacy Policy: Alliance Assault: Cold War Rush

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Alliance Assault: Cold War Rush ("the game") is published by SimplifyBytes ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles.

What the game itself stores

Your progress (level, coins, upgrades, chosen powers, settings and personal records) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers for the game, you do not create an account, and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner, optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • IP address (which gives an approximate location),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, crashes related to ads).

Google uses this data as described in its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data. The only third party that receives data through the game is Google, for advertising as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
